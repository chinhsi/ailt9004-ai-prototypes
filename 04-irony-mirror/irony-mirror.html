    The prompt is the lesson plan

    Everything the AI does comes from this text. Change the tone (gentler for Primary), the number of questions, the irony types, or the image style. The words {lang} and {level} are filled from Settings. Blank = default.

    How to use it in class (10–15 minutes)

    1. Explore: show one word (e.g. 孝順). Ask the class to describe Face A and Face B before reading the captions.
    2. Name the gap: what is said vs what is done. Point at the irony type tag.
    3. Students write one ironic sentence (box under the questions); read three aloud.
    4. Game: 6 words, pairs pick the ironic picture and write the gap in one sentence. Score is only for the picking; the sentence is judged by people.
    5. Ethics minute: who decided what counts as "ironic"? Whose scenes got drawn? (The AI drew a Hong Kong flat because the prompt said so — change it and see.)
