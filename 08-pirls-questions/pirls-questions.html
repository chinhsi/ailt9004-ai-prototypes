Process coverage

PIRLS reports two lower processes (retrieve, straightforward inference) and two higher ones (interpret and integrate, evaluate and critique). A set that is all retrieval looks like a quiz, not a reading test.

Check questions you wrote yourself

Paste your own questions (one per line). The tool says which PIRLS process each one actually measures, whether a reader could answer it from the passage above, what is wrong with it, and how to push it up one process.
