# Reading Comprehension: The Star Ferry

## Questions

### Literal Questions
1. In what year did the Star Ferry begin carrying passengers between Hong Kong Island and Kowloon?
2. What colors are the Star Ferry boats painted?
3. What do the members of the ferry crew wear?
4. How long does a single trip on the Star Ferry take?

### Inferential Questions
5. Why were people in 1898 more dependent on the Star Ferry than commuters are today?
6. Why do office workers "pretend not to notice" the famous view while riding the ferry?
7. Why do some passengers prefer taking the ferry over faster modern transport like tunnels and trains?

### Personal Response Question
8. If you were travelling between Hong Kong Island and Kowloon, would you choose the Star Ferry or the MTR? Give two reasons to support your choice.

---

## Answer Key

1. 1898.
2. Green and white.
3. Blue sailor uniforms.
4. Less than ten minutes.
5. In 1898, there were no cross-harbour tunnels or MTR trains, so the Star Ferry was the only way to cross Victoria Harbour.
6. Office workers ride the ferry regularly as part of their daily routine, so the view is familiar to them, or they are busy reading news on their phones.
7. Passengers enjoy the scenic view, fresh air, and peaceful break ("short pause") from the fast-paced city life.
8. **(Open)** Accept any logical answer with appropriate reasons (e.g., choosing MTR for speed and efficiency, or Star Ferry for the scenic view, cheap fare, and relaxing experience).
