# Vocabulary Study: The Star Ferry

## Target Vocabulary

1. **harbour** · n. · a sheltered area of water next to land where ships and boats can stay safely.
   *Sentence:* Every day, thousands of people cross Victoria Harbour on the Star Ferry.

2. **passenger** · n. · a person who is travelling in a vehicle, boat, or train but not driving or working on it.
   *Sentence:* The green and white boats have carried passengers between Hong Kong Island and Kowloon since 1898.

3. **reflect** · v. · to throw back light or an image off a surface like water or glass.
   *Sentence:* From the open deck you can see the tall buildings on both sides of the harbour, and at night the lights reflect on the water.

4. **sailor** · n. · a person who works as a member of the crew on a ship or boat.
   *Sentence:* The crew wear blue sailor uniforms.

5. **pier** · n. · a platform built out into the water for boarding or leaving boats.
   *Sentence:* When the ferry arrives, a sailor throws a thick rope to the pier and pulls the boat in.

6. **gangway** · n. · a movable plank or ramp used to get on or off a ship or boat.
   *Sentence:* The gangway goes down with a loud bang, and everyone hurries off.

7. **old-fashioned** · adj. · belonging to or typical of a time in the past; not modern.
   *Sentence:* Some people say the ferry is slow and old-fashioned.

8. **pause** · n. · a temporary stop or break in action or activity.
   *Sentence:* In a city that never stops, a ferry ride is a short pause: a few minutes of wind, waves and open sky between two busy shores.

## Vocabulary Practice

Complete the following sentences using the correct words from above. Change word forms if necessary.

1. After working non-stop for hours, the workers needed a brief __________ to rest and drink tea.
2. The cruise ship was crowded with excited __________ who were eager to explore the island.
3. The boat tied up to the wooden __________ so that people could safely disembark.
4. On a calm night, you can see the bright city lights __________ on the smooth surface of the water.
5. The crew lowered the wooden __________ so that passengers could walk onto the boat.

---

## Answer Key

1. pause
2. passengers
3. pier
4. reflect / reflecting
5. gangway
