# Teacher Notes: The Star Ferry

## Target Level & Rationale
* **Target Level:** Key Stage 3 (Secondary 1 – Secondary 3)
* **Rationale:** The source text provides an authentic, culturally relevant context (Hong Kong transport heritage) with accessible language structures and rich vocabulary. It is ideal for junior secondary students to build vocabulary related to transport, history, and city life while practicing literal and inferential comprehension.

---

| Time | Phase | Focus & Activity | File Used |

## Differentiation Strategies

* **Support for Weaker Students:**
  * Provide visual prompts (images of harbour, gangway, pier) before the vocabulary exercise.
  * Allow students to work in pairs for the inferential comprehension questions (Q5–Q7) and offer sentence frames (e.g., *"In 1898, people relied on the ferry because..."*).

* **Extension for Stronger Students:**
  * Ask students to write a short comparison paragraph (approx. 60–80 words) comparing the Star Ferry with the Peak Tram or MTR, incorporating at least 3 target vocabulary words.

---

## Check Before Use

Before using this lesson pack with your class, please review and verify the following:

1. **Factual Updates:** Star Ferry ticket fares and operational details change over time. Clarify to students that the phrase *"costs less than a cup of milk tea"* is a comparative expression reflecting its affordability relative to local culture.
2. **Cultural References:** Ensure students are familiar with basic Hong Kong landmarks mentioned in the text (Victoria Harbour, Kowloon, Hong Kong Island, milk tea).
3. **Vocabulary Level:** Depending on your students' baseline English proficiency (S1 vs S3), check if specialized words like *gangway* or *pier* require additional visual scaffolding.
