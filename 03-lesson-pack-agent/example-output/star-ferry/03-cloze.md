### Passage

Why? First, it is cheap. A trip costs **(3)** __________ than a cup of milk tea. Second, the view is famous. Tourists take photos, **(5)** __________ office workers read the news on their phones and pretend not to notice.

The crew wear blue **(6)** __________ uniforms. When the ferry arrives, a sailor throws a thick rope to the **(7)** __________ and pulls the boat in. The gangway goes down with a loud bang, and everyone hurries off. The whole trip takes less **(8)** __________ ten minutes.
