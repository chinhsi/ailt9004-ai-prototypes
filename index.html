AILT9004 — AI prototypes for language teaching

Small working examples built for the HKU course AILT9004. Free services, no VPN needed in Hong Kong.

  • Speaking Pal — voice conversation partner (open in Chrome or Edge; needs a free OpenRouter key). Read me
  • AI Essay Grader — rubric-based scoring and feedback inside a Google Sheet (paste one file into Apps Script; needs a free OpenRouter key). Not a web page, so there is nothing to click here: follow the 10-minute setup in the README.
  • Lesson Pack Agent — one command turns any text into vocabulary, questions, cloze, quiz and teacher notes; the whole "program" is plain text you can read and edit. Runs in the OpenCode CLI with its free models. Download the folder (40 KB) — the setup guide is inside it.
  • Irony Mirror 反諷鏡 — one word, two faces: sincere vs ironic, with AI-written captions and AI-drawn pictures; Spot-the-irony game (needs a free OpenRouter key; pictures need no key). Read me
  • Picture Talk 看圖說話 — a game: the AI hides a sentence and draws it; describe the picture, get marked, keep your streak (same free keys). Read me
  • 认字乐园 Hanzi Game — phone-first character game (six question types, spaced repetition, recorded voice) built from a one-page teacher note; no key needed. Read me
  • 字靈冒險 Hanzi Quest — Chinese word battles with listening and pinyin questions, whole-word audio, XP and levels; no key, no live AI calls. Read me
  • 閱讀理解出題器 Reading Question Generator — paste a reading passage and pick a framework (PIRLS 2021 四層次 or 祝新華六層次閱讀能力), and get a question set with marks, answer keys and scoring guides; printable student sheet and answer key; a second tab tells you what your own questions actually measure. Interface in English or 中文 (needs a free OpenRouter key). Read me
